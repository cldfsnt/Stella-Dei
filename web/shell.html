God tools · deliberate interventions

Select a patch first.

Wave pushes existing water into up to six neighboring patches: at most 25% of this patch’s water, capped at 1 m equivalent. No water creation or terrain erosion.

Lightning adds a local heat impulse of up to 40°C (capped at 500 K). This is an external god power, not a full lightning simulation. Life responds through actual temperature and flooding rules over time, not guaranteed destruction.

Controls and field notes

Turquoise mats mark real microbial biomass. Tap Habitat to find shallow water; Seed introduces an existing colony. Nutr. + is a deliberate external nutrient input, not food created by the ecosystem.

Microbes need 0.2–50m water, usable light, suitable temperature and resources. Inspect shows carbon biomass, nutrients and the dominant living lineage. Descendants can develop aquatic producer forms: cushion, rosette, ribbon, branching and canopy. Shapes follow inherited traits and actual support tissue; these are not terrestrial plant species or animals.

Habitat care: Use Find habitat → Shape nursery → Seed colony → Watch life for a shallow starting patch. Nursery shapes a 5 m center beside a 15 m reservoir and dry rim; it supplies water once, not new organisms. Press Play if paused. The older level 15 m basin is still available in Life snapshot. Inspect shallow patches as water changes. For routine replenishment, a Water + tap adds 1 m to the selected patch. For precision, open Life snapshot and choose Set 0.5 m. Both supply water once: there is no automatic refill or guaranteed evolution.

Read the map: tan is bare land, blue is water, green is vegetation, and orange dots mark living rabbits. Close-up sprouts show real vegetated patches, not individual plant counts; they grow with coverage and shrink when grazed. Rabbit models stand on the surface when you zoom close.

1. Find your land. Tap Land below the game to jump to dry ground. Use ＋ to get close and Globe to return to a whole-planet view.

2. Shape a habitat. Use Land +/− and Water +/−. Dry land supports rabbits; deep water does not. Pause while sculpting. Water and land brushes respond to how long you hold, not simulation speed. Inspect shows water depth.

3. Plant the land. Choose Plants and paint patches of vegetation. Rabbits eat it, so establish food before adding animals.

4. Add rabbits. Choose Rabbits, then tap suitable land. Watch the population and inspect the surface temperature. This prototype currently has vegetation and rabbits, not a full multi-species ecosystem.

Always daylight keeps the side you are viewing lit while the climate simulation continues. You can change the view in the planet menu.

Tap menu buttons and the tool buttons inside the game. Choose a tool, then touch the planet to use it.

Pinch with two fingers to zoom smoothly. In Navigate mode, drag one finger to explore and tap to use a menu or selected tool. Switch to Paint to brush plants or terrain by dragging. Pinching never finishes as a tool tap. Zoom +/− also works.

Landscape gives you more room. Full screen hides the browser bars. The game automatically resizes when you rotate.

More → Save / Load: Save keeps one planet in this browser on this device, including its ecosystem. It is not a cloud backup. Wait for the Saved message before closing. Clearing site data or using a private browsing session can remove it. Load replaces your unsaved world.

Mobile detail: New browser planets use a lighter grid for smoother play and smaller saves. Existing saved worlds keep their original grid detail when loaded.

Pause, settings and tool details are now in the game, above the planet. Open More → Speed / fast-forward to choose a target of 1, 6 or 24 simulated hours per real second. The top pace button also cycles these targets. Achieved speed is measured separately and may be lower on your device. New worlds start with the 6-hour target; saved worlds keep their requested pace. Pause to sculpt or inspect. Faster time also dries water faster, so watch your habitat.
