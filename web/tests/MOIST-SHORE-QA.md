# Experimental moist-shore producer physiology

This is a bounded aquatic-to-moist-shore biofilm adaptation, not vascular plants, soil, roots, or animal evolution. It adds no saved fields. Actual inherited toughness (`structure`) and paid support tissue determine protection. Existing toughness efficiency cost and support maintenance remain. Zero liquid water never becomes hidden moisture; atmospheric vapor and historical rainfall are not substituted for soil water.

`shoreProtection` multiplies normalized toughness and actual support fraction; `shoreCapable` reports protection at least 0.7, not guaranteed survival. `habitatFitness` returns normal aquatic fitness or conditional moist-film fitness below 20 cm, limited to liquid-temperature conditions. Mortality and photosynthetic capacity change continuously with protection and film hydration. `dispersalHabitat` permits donor-funded offspring to arrive at real moist film, including mutation during reproduction; offspring physiology determines establishment or death. The ordinary Seed action remains aquatic-only.

## Model acceptance

- All 26 microbe tests passed, including the prior 22 aquatic tests.
- All 41 save/model tests passed; schema unchanged.
- Tissue-less and toughness-free ancestors do not photosynthesize on film.
- Zero water, deep water and unsuitable temperature reject the new shore growth pathway.
- Support, carbon and nutrient accounting remain conservative under film growth followed by drying.
- Nonadapted donor-funded propagules die on film, with exact initial donor-to-offspring mass transfer.
- Ordinary seeded aquatic founders, production growth and unforced mutation, and real paid dispersal yielded a shore descendant with growth/reproductive credit in a suitable controlled 5 cm film. No biological traits were assigned. This is an environment-controlled model check, NOT full-world hydrological proof.

The first attempted parent-phenotype dispersal gate blocked new frontier adaptations. It was replaced with an environmental arrival gate, while actual offspring phenotype still governs growth and death. No forced mutations or free survivors were introduced.

Do not market this as playable terrestrial progression based only on controlled tests. A conserved soil-water model would be separate future work requiring an explicit save design.


## Actual coupled-world result: not a playable progression yet

Generated world seed 1337; one ordinary Seed and final 15 m basin; at day 20 an explicit diagnostic-only terrain intervention raised one adjacent floor cell to near local water surface, with one real 15 cm water-target action. No biological assignments, repeat watering, forced mutation, or injected survival. This fine shelf action is **not exposed in the shipped UI**.

Over days 21–40, actual film persisted around 1.58–2.10 cm (2.05 cm at day 40), but the shore cell had **zero living biomass on every daily sample**, no shore-capable residents, and no demonstrated establishment. Thus sustained water existed in this test, but natural aquatic descendants did not establish there. The actual world does not pass the desired playable terrestrial progression acceptance check. Save invalid-rejection/exact restoration passed.

The model should remain an experimental checkpoint, not a feature advertised as completed or shipped with the next interface-only release. Further work needs accessible shore/soil mechanics and evidence of genuine ecological colonization. Do not turn this failure into hidden irrigation, altered offspring traits, or a timer-based unlock.
