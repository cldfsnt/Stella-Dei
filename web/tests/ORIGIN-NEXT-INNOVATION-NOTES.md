# Read-only next-stage notes (not production work)

The first Origin release must first pass unstabilized climate/water starter acceptance. Controlled growth and save tests are not enough. Current diagnostics show that habitat selection and water/thermal behavior can dominate biology; do not cover this with a new evolution badge.

## Actual bottlenecks before further stages

1. Two cohort slots eventually fill. New donor-funded lineages are rejected rather than corrupting existing populations, but a closed patch then cannot explore further trait combinations until a lineage disappears or a suitable neighboring cell opens. Mutation opportunities are spatially limited.
2. Natural nutrient stock limits local living carbon before the geometric carrying capacity. More nutrients must be an explicit external input; continually granting nutrients as a reward would fake the ecology.
3. Suitable shallow cells may have few suitable neighbors, and initially warm water can chill or dry under the real climate solver. A player must see water, temperature and light conditions, not only biomass.
4. Current models track living parent links, not an unlimited phylogenetic archive. Apparent elaborate lineage trees would overstate recorded history.

## A defensible next functional innovation

After environment stability is addressed, add a bounded temporary mutant nursery or modest third/fourth cohort capacity, with strict donor-funded biomass and resource conservation. A rare variant must compete and increase its actual population share; never overwrite an entire established cohort just because a random threshold is crossed. Compare lineage diversity, memory cost and performance before picking the representation.

Then prototype a heritable structural/cooperative allocation trait. More structure costs growth/maintenance and reduces immediate reproduction, but can reduce desiccation loss or permit slightly shallower wet habitat. The first multicellular-producer-like form should therefore have a testable ecological niche, not simply a bigger sprite. A stable lineage carrying that strategy must emerge and reproduce under appropriate pressure; high-structure cells should lose to cheaper cells in benign water. Explicitly call this an authored game abstraction of functional innovation, not a scientific simulation of the origin of multicellularity.

Only after that, model an early consumer strategy with real prey biomass consumption, conversion efficiency, oxygen demand and reproduction budgets. Introduce an appropriate simple grazer, not instant rabbits. A consumer without prey must decline; adding it must measurably alter producer biomass. Existing rabbit mechanics remain a separate sandbox illustration.

## Tests for the next model step

- Mutant lineage is an actual descendant funded from a parent's resources.
- Stress/control comparison shows the tradeoff: protection helps under drying but costs fitness in benign water.
- Structural trait persists through reproduction and exact save/load.
- No extra biomass or nutrients are created by phenotype change.
- Successful transition changes habitat use or food-web behavior in actual rendered play.
- No automatic unlock or guaranteed success counters; a dispersal tool becomes available only for an actually existing lineage.

No implementation of these innovations has begun.
