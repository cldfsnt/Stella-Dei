# Experimental shore follow-up: 100 days

Same deterministic world seed 1337, ordinary initial Seed, real 15 m basin and one diagnostic-only shelf/water action on day 20. No extra biological changes, repeat watering or forced mutations. The isolated experimental build was retained while active release source returned to v17.

The shoreline retained liquid film throughout daily samples from days 21–100: initially 1.58 cm, ending 2.29 cm. Nevertheless biomass was zero at every daily sample; no shore-capable residents or successful colonization were demonstrated. The entire world remained biologically active. The test therefore establishes that this diagnostic shelf can retain water, but not that the new physiology produces a usable shoreline evolution path.

The source patch had one surviving cohort at day 40 with biomass 0.155 and support 0.00304. Production reproduction preferentially uses local empty slots; turnover may limit dispersal. Current diagnostic does not record every attempted propagule arrival or hourly source reproductive credit, so this is a plausible bottleneck, not a proven sole cause.

Invalid-save rejection and exact restoration passed after 100 days. Keep this model experimental. Next investigation should instrument actual dispersal attempts and offspring survival under real fluxes before changing ecology or presenting terrestrial progression as complete. Fine shelf creation remains a diagnostic intervention not an exposed player action.
