## Actual finite-water law and tissue constraint

`10 * (support * root_fraction) * soil * dt / 86400`.

`.1 * frond_mass * dt / 86400 * (.5+.5*light) * (1−immersion) * activity`.
Actual paid flow is demand-capped and shares a finite source with evaporation/other roots. Final hydration is lineage-owned credit divided by max(entry,current final-depth demand), downward rounded; it is not a free trait multiplier.
