How to read this ecosystem

Colony IDs are serial identifiers, not a count of living colonies or species. Births since start includes colonies that later died. Generation counts descent steps from a seeded ancestor. Parent IDs may refer to colonies no longer alive. Net change includes births, losses, movement and interventions.

Watch reports actual photosynthesis, grazing and biological carbon returned to air. The return excludes movement respiration and god tools; this is not a complete carbon budget.
