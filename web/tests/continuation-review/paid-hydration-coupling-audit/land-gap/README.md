# Moist-land establishment gap audit

Read-only assessment of the experimental retention-coupled copy, 2026-11-15. Not a production feature or prediction of successful establishment.

## Existing opportunities

`Planet.simulateMicrobes` admits paid exploratory propagules into any finite depth/temperature destination. This is not restricted by the aquatic `habitable` predicate. The actual child inherits a funded fraction of parental stored water; lower capacity returns excess. `hydration_exchange.step` obtains replenishment only by actual represented withdrawal from current liquid, after the physical atmosphere/water stage. Natural condensation adds real surface liquid, which can therefore be used next biological step. Continuous effective hydration permits photosynthetic activity and reduced exposure losses in dry/thin-film conditions, subject to temperature exclusions, paid support, nutrients, carbon, oxygen and space. These are genuine opportunities, not guaranteed fitness.

## Hard gates and missing mechanisms

- Larger fallback daughter placement still requires aquatic `habitable`: 0.2–50m and temperature bounds. Exploration is the route across this boundary, not general reproduction targeting.
- Autonomous relocation sheds support and returns all stored water at source. It is not a water-carrying terrestrial migration mechanism.
- Uptake requires represented surface liquid. There is no soil pore-water, capillary uptake, direct vapor harvesting or interception reservoir. The rainfall diagnostic is not a separate water source.
- Capacity requires paid support and nonzero retention allocation. A newly positive mutation does not fill added capacity for free.
- Every living cohort loses stored water through the fixed experimental evaporation law; offspring share existing stores. No dormancy or seed bank exists. Below .0025 biomass the existing establishment cutoff recycles a colony; no tiny-spore survival should be claimed.
- The coarse approximately 87.6km patch width does not resolve coastal microsites or moist soil. A nearshore neighbor may be deep ocean, not a suitable land niche.
- Existing physical water drift and omitted latent heat remain limitations. Local exchange conservation does not establish global thermodynamic closure.

## Smallest next experiment if the six preregistered arms fail

Do not add a trait, increase mutation rates, refill water or select successful destinations. Continue the same frozen endpoint/config for one fixed additional horizon, with a bounded observer for ALL naturally born positive-allocation descendants. Record actual surface-liquid charging, inherited water, arrival depth/temperature, subsequent dry exposure, depletion, paid births and cutoff losses. Compare matched mutation-off keys already preregistered. Distinguish no exposure opportunity, no useful mutant, failure to charge, failed dispersal and physiological failure. A zero-positive-lineage result is informative and not a request to tune rates. Only demonstrated liquid-access limitation would justify a separately preregistered conservative substrate-water experiment; that would require a new physical pool, exchange/transport and save semantics, not a free hydration multiplier.

Self-sustaining moist-land establishment requires multiple actual paid generations whose water/resources come from the modeled environment, not just founder survival on inherited stores or transient shallow-water growth. No such result is asserted here.
