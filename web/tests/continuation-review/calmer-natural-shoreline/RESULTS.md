## Practical implication

The ordinary aquatic-to-shoreline community is already visibly active and survives the fixed disturbance. The land-establishment gap is that support/root mutations arrive AFTER early drawdown and remain underwater elsewhere, while a dry patch loses life. Next meaningful player intervention should use an actually living supported-root descendant and a finite moist margin, not assign roots or globally increase mutation. Do not repeat or select that intervention until separately scoped. Current useful feedback should distinguish 'has inherited roots' from 'currently exposed and using soil water'; a10mm target is not a promise to stay10mm or to expose millimetre-sized life.
