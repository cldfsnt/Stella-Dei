# One next functional experiment: donor-funded directed sampling

Read-only proposal. No production edits, new physiology, saved fields or simulation runs. Investigated after the compact Life-panel checkpoint68cc188.

## Player consequence
Let the player select an existing evolved colony and deliberately place a paid, nonmutating daughter into one neighboring patch. Seed currently introduces ancestors; this would let the player actually test an earned producer in a prepared wet versus shallow niche. Parent loses existing biomass/support and earned reproduction credit. Child competes and can die normally. Label the operation a **player-directed sample**, not natural dispersal, an evolved mobility trait, a species unlock or guaranteed establishment.

Use existing reproduction semantics as the starting experiment, not a whole-cohort teleport. `Microbes.reproduce(..., mutate=false)` requires parent biomass≥0.03 and credit≥0.04, transfers0.01 tissue including proportional support, gives child new ID/parent/generation and zero inherited feeding/movement histories, and debits the donor. Destination must have a free slot and sufficient occupancy capacity. Nonmutation preserves the selected phenotype without RNG rerolls. No added water, nutrients, CO2, O2 or immunity. Retain ecological cost: the child comes out of its parent's actual tissue and resources, not a copy minted from the UI.

A whole-cohort copy is not a shortcut: it would need careful treatment of biological relocation history and propagule observers. Copying a cohort while retaining the source would create matter. A player tool must not increment autonomous relocation records or imply free evolved locomotion.

## Why this rather than speculative low-demand dormancy
Existing support is paid tissue: it reduces collecting biomass, respires finite oxygen for upkeep, changes competition for the existing light budget and contributes to shallow-film protection. Inherited structure also reduces wet uptake and changes mortality. These are actual model tradeoffs. Prior reciprocal assays demonstrate habitat-dependent phenotype-package reversal, but wet outcomes include grazing and the shallow-origin phenotype sometimes produces MORE funded daughters. Do not sell the wet winner as universally faster breeding or attribute the result solely to structure. No dormant pool or generalized basal energy budget has been validated; fixed-cohort low-demand experiments do not justify a new strategy's fitness claim.

Current support upkeep is oxygen-limited; missing oxygen reduces paid upkeep without an independently modelled repair deficit. Do not use that omission to promise an anoxic persistence advantage. This sampling feature exposes existing behavior without inventing one.

## Bounded experiment before any UI
1. Transaction gate on copied production data: one eligible actual evolved donor, adjacent empty target, one successful nonmutating reproduction. Confirm exact trait values, child ancestry, source debit, support partition, credit debit, finite unchanged atmosphere and no mutation-RNG advancement. Failure cases: stale donor ID/slot/world/target, same-cell destination, nonneighbor, insufficient mass/credit, full target, near-full target, lineage overflow, invalid numeric state. Refusal must be atomic and not spend credit.
2. **Numerical gate is real.** Existing reproduceSized uses separate f32 parent subtraction and child assignment. Prior accounting research found measurable accumulated f32 drift. Measure represented source loss versus destination gain and nutrient-bound mass. Do not claim bit-exact conservation or relax tolerance after a failure. If a new public action cannot obey a documented ULP-bound transfer budget, stop and fix a narrowly shared transfer primitive in isolation first. Do not adopt fixed-cohort residual arrays for inherited children: those are not ownership-safe under births/movement.
3. Use original saved evolved donors from three existing seed histories, retaining actual tissue fraction and all inherited traits. Test wet and shallow donors separately; neither is an assumed winner. Two destination environments (1m versus8cm), no magical dry-land suitability gate; player may choose harsh habitat with a warning. If controlled temperatures/depths are held constant, call it a laboratory fixture, not a coupled-world result.
4. Matched branches restore the SAME source+target world. Sampling versus no-action control must start with identical total C/N/O2, donor/target resources, RNG and local community. The sample redistributes tissue and spends credit; that difference is the treatment. Across habitat branches, only the declared water intervention differs; track its externally added/removed water. Do not top up depleted food or seed more cohorts during the trial.
5. Swap cohort slots to check order dependence. Include empty and occupied destinations; neither an empty patch nor high structure implies survival. Fixed-duration10-day outcomes: donor mass/output cost, sampled daughter's actual fate/peak biomass and whether it funds its own descendant, resident biomass, fixation/respiration, total live+dead+gas carbon and bound/free nutrient. Trace lineage ID through real relocation; absence from the target alone is NOT death. Use the actual scheduler rather than exporting offspring into an inert ledger.
6. Require at least one actual generated-world player-flow check using earned eligible donor mass/credit. Do not assign credit to make a supposedly natural sample available. A “not ready to sample” result is useful evidence, not permission to fabricate eligibility. Stop if meaningful availability is too rare for a usable tool; do not force success by lowering thresholds until it works.

## Fairness and success criteria
This experiment tests a player intervention and conservation, not proof of improved biological fitness. Do not require a favored phenotype to win. Success is a legitimate, affordable action with understandable donor cost, honest habitat-dependent outcomes and fully traceable daughter fate, including failed establishments. Reject duplicate matter, free mutation rerolls, state changes on failed previews, stale target execution, invented survival, or source/target baselines that differ in unreported resources. Existing0.001 multi-day ledger tolerance is historical approximate evidence only; transaction precision must be evaluated separately.

If gates pass, use two-step explicit donor/destination selection with a receipt: source colony ID, donor tissue/credit spent, destination patch and “survival not guaranteed.” Refresh rechecks current ownership and resources; cancel/load/new selection clears pending action. No automatic pause/speed change. UI/source integration needs a separate assignment after these tests; nothing is implemented here.
