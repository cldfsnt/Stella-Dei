# Frozen actual-engine feeding cost/benefit diagnostic

Approved by parent before execution. Four independent deep restores of the natural day60 world, then 600 coupled 1440-second ticks each (10 simulated days), current v54 production engine except two isolated QA switches:
0 normal; 1 grazing disabled, inherited feeding costs retained; 2 grazing enabled, feeding-specific costs disabled; 3 both disabled.
Cost removal affects only the production photosynthesis multiplier (1−0.7*feeding) and the +0.10*feeding wet/film mortality term. Dry mortality is unchanged. No trait, starting biomass, support, credit, nutrient, gas, water, temperature, RNG or reproduction assignments. Cost-off is intentionally unphysical diagnostic ablation, never a candidate for production. It can change resource uptake rather than directly gifting resources; quantify actual receipts. No pace hypothesis imported.

Primary question: within the same starting world, what are the effects and interaction of the actual feeding benefit and paid costs? Not a causal claim about the superiority of two whole phenotypes. All cohorts/world statistics retained. RNG trajectories diverge naturally after intervention; no paired identical individual stochastic histories claimed.
