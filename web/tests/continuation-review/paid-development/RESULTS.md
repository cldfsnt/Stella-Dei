# Paid newborn development: bounded feasibility, not a new law

## Decision

Do not promote or build a new saved-law branch from this result. Partitioning already-paid newborn carbon into tissue removes the zero-support dependency, but the three actual root mutants do not demonstrate plausible dry-ground net growth. Tiny root investment remains insufficient even under the declared favorable dry control. No world run, phenotype search, parameter change or subsequent allocation was tried.

## Contract and scope

Test-only `develop(child, enabled)` sets support to downward-rounded `child.biomass * child.organization` at hypothetical birth. Total child biomass, C/N, donor debit, ancestry, random stream and reproductive credit are unchanged. Root tissue remains `support * root_fraction`, and leaves become `biomass-support`. It is a developmental partition of the paid packet, NOT creation of structural carbon, hydration or a live-colony conversion. At organization below the inherited support ratio it also reduces support and increases leaves. Real existing maintenance and growth opportunity costs apply.

This is not a physically calibrated tissue-synthesis model: no extra differentiation energy/time has been introduced. Nor is the test-only transformation integrated into a birth transaction or saved format. Failure sinks remain unchanged because no child is developed on a failed launch. Any later integrated implementation would need a prospective law and all-route atomicity review; none is justified by this screen alone.

## Reproduction and accounting gates

Five focused native ReleaseSafe tests plus imported source tests passed. Controlled organization0/.025/.25/.7 and child budgets .004/.01/.02 cover zero, below/equal/above an inherited .25 support ratio. Exact disabled return preserves the complete cohort. Three actual reproduction routes (local propagule, large local birth, unequal-area wind arrival) retain donor, stats, child carbon and nitrogen; only the receiver's support partition changes. Failed actual wind launch preserves absence of child, real detritus/mineral receipt and donor RNG. No extra water, root credit, or offspring credit is created. Historical production files were copied unchanged; this is not a new full-scheduler historical parity run.

## Fixed physiological probes

Inputs are the FIRST recorded mortality rows for actual root mutants421,213,125, not their later endpoint phenotypes. All were born with B=.00400000019 and support0, root1/32. The log retains each source row. All three phenotypes are probed at their recorded budget and two declared representative budgets .01/.02, with current parent-ratio zero support versus proposed organization partition.

Scenario0 uses recorded temperature/depth/light. Scenario1 is explicitly hypothetical dry ground at each genotype's optimum, light1, full finite soil1kg/m². Both have finite C=.02, mineral N=.02, oxygen capacity=.1; use the unchanged real shared-soil solver (conductance10, area1e10, vapor1, no bare request) and one unchanged1440-second physiological step with the paid receipt/owner. There is NO intervening atmosphere/column, so this is a controlled local feasibility test, not a natural-world forecast or long-term survival result. Resource supply is an assay environment, not a player grant.

At actual .004C birth:

- ID421 organization0: no partition, no root tissue/hydration. Favorable dry net −.000241698C per24min, exactly unchanged.
- ID213 organization.0502883: support .000201153C; roots .00000628604C; leaves .00379885C. Actual dry conditions fund h=.204580, growth .00000980985C versus mortality .000212277C, net −.000203223C. Favorable dry daylight h=.165472, growth .0000182928C versus mortality .000221192C, net −.000203670C. Lower water demand under dim light raises funded fraction but does not supply light or survival.
- ID125 organization.0350528: its recorded27.4m submergence needs no roots; development instead reduces leaves and incurs maintenance. Hypothetical dry h=.113519 does not offset mortality. All three budget sizes remain net-negative on the favorable dry test.

The generous pre-competition dry flow/demand bound is approximately `h <= min(1,100*s*r*soil/((1-s)*(0.5+0.5*light)))`, with support fraction s and root fraction r. At light1/full soil, s=.0502883,r=1/32 gives .16547; s=.0350528 gives .11352. Finite shared soil, final-demand ownership, cold gates and hydrological changes can only invalidate this simple favorable estimate, not guarantee it in a world.

## Practical interpretation

Organization+root allocation is not equivalent to functional land establishment. Paid development can create a little functional tissue, yet does not solve this observed demographic bottleneck. It also imposes a real aquatic cost. Keep existing explicit paid rooted/hardy founders and existing natural variation honest. A future player journey using an actually inherited better-funded root phenotype may be more informative than shipping this isolated developmental rule. Do not speed mutations, lower establishment thresholds or extend this screen to find a winner.
