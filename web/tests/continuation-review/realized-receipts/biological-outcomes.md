## No slow invasion demonstrated

## Recommendation
Do not introduce an inheritable pace trait on the strength of isolated slow-population survival. The shared-resource evidence supports transient scarcity resistance paid for by weaker recovery, but does not yet support meaningful coexistence, rare invasion or slower-strategy succession. Existing production generation/feeding/support variation should not be relabeled as this hypothetical trait. A future model needs a specific mechanistic hypothesis (e.g. paid environmental switching with recovery lag) and a clear gameplay purpose, not another coefficient sweep until slow wins. Current data justify preserving the research and returning to actual natural-world establishment loss and stable numerical accounting.
