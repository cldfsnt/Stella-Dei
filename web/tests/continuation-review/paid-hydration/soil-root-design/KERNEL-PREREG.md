# Parameterized local transfer kernel: proposed acceptance contract

Design checkpoint before code/outcomes; awaiting independent contract review. No production physiology, scheduler, save schema or calibrated rates. Goal is conservative local plumbing and paid-allocation accounting, not fitness or land establishment.

Inputs are finite f64 water densities(kg/m²): surface, soil, vapor and four cohort stores; biomass/support carbon and allocation fractions; positive dt(seconds); caller-supplied substrate capacity, infiltration/evaporation/drainage rate limits(kg/m²/s), root conductance(kgWater/kgRootC/s), and each cohort's potential transpiration demand(kg/m² for this interval). These are explicit test parameters, not proposed game defaults. Native f32 pool conversion remains a later separate receipt adapter.

Validate before mutation: nonnegative finite material quantities/rates/capacity, support<=biomass, root/storage fractions each[0,1] with sum<=1, stores within actual paid storage capacity, finite dt>0 and derived limits. Roots=support*u; reservoir tissue=support*r; aboveground support=support−root−reservoir. Do not add carbon or apply simulated maintenance/growth in this kernel. Paid carbon partition is an accounting output; future actual core must charge its existing upkeep and geometry loss.

Proposed deterministic order: represented surface→soil infiltration bounded by capacity/rate; soil→vapor bare evaporation bounded by availability/rate; simultaneous root demands share remaining actual soil, bounded by potential demand and root tissue×conductance×wetness×dt; drainage returns residual soil→surface bounded by rate. This priority order is an explicit operator-splitting assumption to review, not a measured hydraulic model. No within-step reinfiltration loop or infinite cycling.

First kernel does NOT consume/fill cohort storage. Root-only throughput transfers actual soil water directly to vapor and reports delivered_i / positive demand_i; demand0 gives signal0. Caller must not also spend the same delivered water as storage or evaporation. Existing storage and future combined signal are separate, unresolved physiology integration. Fixed habitat/temperature gates are not changed here; reported root signal alone is not permission to grow on dry terrain.

All proposed transactions staged in local copies. Actual represented source differences must fund actual represented receiver increments. Exact-or-refuse gate with strict arithmetic/loss-detection; no epsilon credit or protected compensator introduced. A refused transfer leaves material participants unchanged and is reported, not hidden. Four demands use simultaneous proportional shares independent of slot ordering. Receipts and physical totals audited independently with exact dyadic/rational witnesses, not only a wide floating sum.

Frozen unit fixtures: zero sources/demand/rates/tissue, full soil, dry soil, finite infiltration+evaporation+drainage, demand above/below supply, all24 slot permutations with unequal support/demand, root-only/storage-only/neither partition, exhausted repeated cycles with no refill, invalid/overflow/huge-versus-tiny atomic refusal. Use dyadic quantities for exact expected elementary cases; arbitrary finite scale probes expose explicit refusal coverage without changing policy. A fixed existing supported natural phenotype (root478 naturalday60) supplies carbon/support numbers for a prescribed root allocation demonstration; this is not a naturally evolved root trait and assigns no new biomass. No ecological rates or survival outcome selected.

Acceptance requires exact total water and unchanged carbon partition sum, no capacity or demand overfill, no root delivery without actual tissue+soil, all-slot fairness or explicit atomic refusal, finite bounded signals, and no persistent state/global RNG. Future engine integration must reconcile native pool quantization, root/storage inherited allocation constraints, soil hydrology operator ordering, real habitat combination and timestep response before model enablement.
