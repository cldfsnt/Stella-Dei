# Moist-shore diagnosis and isolated calibration

## Baseline failure is within-day drying, not absent water in daily snapshots

Original local-first reproduction produced one shelf arrival over 100 days. At day 40.30 a genuine child from adjacent aquatic cell arrived with 0.01 biomass, inherited structure 0.1300, support 0.0003501, and protection 0.8668. It grew to 0.01043 during daylight while film was 2.6–4.9 cm. Film then fell to 1.8 mm and zero; it died by day 40.75. Daily samples around 2 cm had aliased the wet portion of this daily cycle. This corrects the earlier interpretation of continuously sustained film.

A separate 25% neighbor-first experiment increased arrivals to three but none had paid support tissue, and none established. This scheduling experiment is not the proposed physiological fix and should not be silently combined into the baseline comparison.

## Dormancy candidate

Protection comes from existing inherited toughness and paid live support tissue. Protected tissue continues reducing desiccation mortality while dry, rather than losing all protection at the instant hydration reaches zero. Carbon fixation still requires actual liquid film and light; zero-water fixation is exactly zero. Ongoing mortality and support upkeep consume biomass; prolonged uninterrupted drought ultimately kills even protected forms. No water, traits or biomass are created.

## Calibration status and limits

Nonlinear desiccation hazard `(1 - protection)^2` plus positive dormant maintenance extended actual shelf survival to roughly 4.8 days but did not establish. Full thermal tracing found a 272.06–315.61 K daily cycle. A brief 19-step frost interval across 290 observed steps switched protection off at the original 273.15 K boundary.

A further approved graded dormant-cold candidate tapers protection toward 260 K, adds cold mortality, keeps zero fixation in frozen film, and preserves thermal growth fitness. All 29 microbe regression/unit tests pass, including eventual uninterrupted frost/drought death. However the 60 measured depth/light/temperature samples replayed periodically still fail sustained-growth acceptance: a canonical strong cohort survives 30 cycles but shrinks from 0.02 to 0.00795. A fixture matching the observed descendant's recorded traits dies; efficiency and mobility were not logged and remain defaults in that fixture, so it is not an exact genotype replay.

These are phenomenological game-ecology parameters, not validated real-species physiology. Do not claim this marginal cold/ephemeral shelf is now a playable evolution path. Do not remove every environmental cost merely to force one founder to thrive. Next comparison should use a genuinely warmer actual habitat or legitimate player climate care, while preserving paid offspring and real environmental fluxes.
