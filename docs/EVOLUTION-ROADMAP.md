# Living-world iteration roadmap

## Acceptance principles
- Begin with simple organisms, then earn new ecological capabilities through inherited variation and actual environmental selection.
- Show usable habitats, actual biological states and consequences. Avoid random-click discovery, permanent timer unlocks, or cosmetic effects presented as simulation.
- Keep portrait touch targets, pinch/navigation, saving and rollback working. Native-engine checks are useful but are not physical Android performance measurements.
- Save experimental failures as well as successful code. Do not merge a feature because only a controlled fixture works.

## Verified foundations
- Origin aquatic inherited traits, growth, reproduction, grazing, relocation, paid support tissue and six map-visible producer morphologies.
- Explicit basin action sustains an ordinary one-Seed path and diversified descendants; no automatic refill or injected evolved organisms.
- Direct habitat/basin/seed/inspect controls; real seed-eligibility zones and actionable failure reasons.
- Separate Sandbox plants and rabbits. This is not yet proof of evolution from microbes into rabbits.

## Active experiments
- Moist-shore producer establishment: preserve actual liquid-water accounting, paid inherited protection and zero photosynthesis without water. Daily sampling hid intraday drying; survival must be verified at simulation-step resolution. Experimental branch `experimental/moist-shore` records controlled success and ordinary-world failures.
- Wave tool: bounded redistribution of existing water, with actual flooding/hydrological consequences and no automatic erosion claim.
- Lightning/heat strike: explicit localized energy input and actual trait-sensitive thermal responses, with clearly labeled simplified physics.

## Next biological strategies
- Drought-tolerant dormancy that still loses resources and eventually dies, not growth from hidden moisture.
- Conserved dormant propagule/seed bank with delayed recruitment, explicit accounting and migration-safe saves.
- Fire-sensitive versus resistant/recruitment-responsive strategies with inherited costs. Fire may release resources or germination cues only through defined state; never spawn plants from a timer or nothing.
- Distinct visible forms tied to functional traits, then a genuine terrestrial and animal transition. Do not label aquatic growth forms as completed land-plant taxonomies.

## Release gates
- Boundary and conservation tests, deterministic replay/save round-trip, realistic player-action harness, ordinary-world survival and an untreated comparison.
- Visual evidence separates controlled rendering fixtures from genuine evolved gameplay.
- User-facing controls explain what changes, what is blocked and why. Preserve the current private audience and recoverable previous deployment.
